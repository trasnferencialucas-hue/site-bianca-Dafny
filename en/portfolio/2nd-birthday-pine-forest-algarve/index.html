Birthday session

Two years old, one balloon and a whole pine forest to run through.

A birthday session at Mata da Conceição, in Tavira. The guest of honour was two years old, had a balloon shaped like a 2 and no intention of sitting still. My job was to keep up.

A birthday session doesn't need a party.

It needs a child, something they love to hold and time without hurry. Here it was a balloon shaped like a two. When hands have something to do, poses stop existing.

I shoot sessions like this in pine forests, on the beach or at home, across the Algarve. They can be just the child or the whole family.
