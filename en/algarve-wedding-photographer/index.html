I photograph weddings and elopements across the Algarve — from countryside estates to barefoot ceremonies on the sand. No endless pose checklists, no one shouting instructions. Just the day happening, with someone paying close attention.

Who it's for

Couples who prefer honest pictures to staged ones — and who want to look at them in twenty years and still recognise themselves. From 120-guest weddings in Lagos to two-person elopements at sunset in Sagres.

What's included
  • Planning meeting and location scouting (in person or online)
  • 6, 8, 10 hours or full-day coverage
  • Golden-hour couple session, away from the guests
  • Every photograph from the day, edited in colour and black and white
  • Private online gallery with downloads and sharing
  • Second shooter and film available on request
  • Delivered in 4 to 6 weeks

Frequently asked
How far in advance should I book?
For weddings between May and September, ideally 8 to 12 months ahead. I take on a limited number of weddings each year so I can give each one my full attention.
Do you travel outside the Algarve?
Yes. Alentejo and Lisbon with minimal extra cost; the rest of Portugal, Spain and international destinations with a travel and accommodation quote.
What if it rains?
Rain in the Algarve is rare — and it makes beautiful pictures. I always carry a plan B of covered locations and clear umbrellas in the car.

Shall we talk?

Tell me the date and place you have in mind. I reply within 24 hours with availability, prices and suggestions.
