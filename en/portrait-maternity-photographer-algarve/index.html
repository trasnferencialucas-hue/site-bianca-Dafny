Individual portraits, maternity and newborn. Calm sessions with no built sets, where the only thing that interests me is what appears on someone's face the moment they forget the camera.

Who it's for

Anyone who needs a professional portrait that doesn't look like a passport photo. Expectant mothers from 28 weeks. Newborns in their first two weeks, photographed at home, in their parents' bed, by window light.

What's included
  • A conversation beforehand about what you want the images to say
  • 45 to 60 minutes of shooting
  • Natural retouching — skin keeps its texture, never plastic
  • Around 30 edited high-resolution photographs
  • Vertical and horizontal crops for social media and LinkedIn
  • Newborn sessions at home, at the baby's pace

Frequently asked
When should I do my maternity session?
Between 28 and 34 weeks. The bump is round and you can still walk comfortably on sand.
Do you do newborn sessions in a studio?
No. I always shoot at home, within the first fifteen days, in natural light and without props. The baby sets the pace.
Do you do corporate headshots?
Yes, individual or team, at your space or outdoors. For teams, request a quote on the Brands page.

Shall we talk?

Tell me the date and place you have in mind. I reply within 24 hours with availability, prices and suggestions.
