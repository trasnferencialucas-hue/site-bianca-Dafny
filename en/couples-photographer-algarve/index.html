Frequently asked
We have no idea how to pose. Is that a problem?
It's the sentence I hear most, and it has never been a problem. I give simple, concrete prompts, walk, lean your forehead in, whisper a story, and photograph what happens in between.
Where's the best place for a couple session in the Algarve?
It depends on the light and the season. In summer I prefer dunes and pine woods, which empty out at the end of the day; off-season, the cliffs of Lagos and Carvoeiro are spectacular. I always suggest two or three options for your date.
Can we bring a change of clothes?
Yes, though it's usually unnecessary. I'd rather you were comfortable in one outfit than lose twenty minutes of light changing.

Shall we talk?

Tell me the date and place you have in mind. I reply within 24 hours with availability, prices and suggestions.
