Frequently asked
Do we need to prepare the space?
I send a preparation checklist a week ahead: linens, flowers, what to clear off the counters. It makes an enormous difference to the result.
Do you photograph food as well?
Yes, in natural light whenever possible and in coordination with the kitchen so dishes arrive at the right moment.
How long does the shoot take?
A three-bedroom villa takes half a day. A small hotel, one day. Larger properties we plan over two days to catch both golden hours.

Shall we talk?

Tell me the date and place you have in mind. I reply within 24 hours with availability, prices and suggestions.
