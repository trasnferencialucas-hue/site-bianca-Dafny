How it works

Conversation

Tell me who you are, what you're celebrating and your possible dates. I reply within 24 hours with availability and prices.

Booking

A simple contract and a 30% deposit. The date is yours and we start planning the place and the right hour of light.

The session

On the day you'll barely notice me. You walk, talk and laugh — I move around you and keep catching it.

Delivery

A preview within 48 hours and a private gallery with high-resolution downloads, ready to print.
